# Merge48 Privacy Policy

_Last updated: June 2026_

Merge48 is an offline puzzle game. It is designed to collect as little data
as possible — in practice, none.

## What we collect

Nothing. Merge48 has no analytics, no advertising SDKs, no crash reporting,
and no server of its own. Your score and best score are stored only on your
device.

## Wallet interaction

If you choose to connect a Solana wallet, the connection happens locally on
your device through Solana Mobile's Mobile Wallet Adapter protocol. The app
sees only the public address your wallet chooses to share, and uses it solely
to display the address and to request a message signature when you ask to
sign your score. Private keys never leave your wallet app. Merge48 does not
transmit your address, your scores, or your signatures anywhere.

## Optional purchases

Merge48 offers optional in-app purchases (Second Chance, Pro Unlock, trophy
NFT minting, tips) paid as Solana transactions from your wallet. When
you start a purchase, the app makes one network request to a public Solana
RPC endpoint to fetch a recent blockhash; the transaction itself is reviewed,
signed, and submitted by your own wallet app. Purchases are recorded on the
public Solana blockchain, as all on-chain transactions are. The app stores
your Pro unlock status on-device only.
